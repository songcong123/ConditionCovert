## 1. González, Hidalgo & Barabási (2008) — Understanding Individual Human Mobility Patterns
- **arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.1256 ｜ Nature 453, 779–782
- **本地**: `Gonzalez2008_human_mobility_arXiv0806.1256.pdf`（12 页）
- **对本项目的意义**：个体域距离分布的"截断幂律"出处——与本项目网约车域 logNormal+机场分量形成"域差异"对照（NYC数据集介绍.md §7 的"域提示"条）

**关键原句**（页码为 arXiv 版）：
| p | 原文 | 中文要点 |
|---|---|---|
| p1 | "in contrast with the random trajectories predicted by the prevailing Lévy flight and random walk models, human trajectories show a high degree of temporal and spatial regularity" | 反驳 Lévy 飞行：人类轨迹高度时空规律 |
| p1 | "individual travel patterns collapse into a single spatial probability distribution" | 个体模式归一化后坍缩到同一分布 |
| p3 | "the radius of gyration distribution … approximated with a truncated power-law P(rg) = (rg + rg0)^−βr · exp(−rg/κ)" | **回转半径 = 截断幂律**（核心公式）|
| p3 | "an ensemble of Lévy agents … is not sufficient to explain the truncated power law distribution P(rg) exhibited by the mobile phone users" | 单纯 Lévy 模型解释不了截断 |
| p4 | "the return probability is characterized by several peaks at 24 h, 48 h, and 72 h, capturing a strong tendency of humans to return to locations they visited before" | **24/48/72 小时回归峰**——时间节律的个体版证据 |
| p5 | "…spending [most] of their time to a few locations, while spending their remaining time in 5 to 50 places" | 时间高度集中于少数地点 |

**关键图**：`figures/Gonzalez2008_p10_fig3.png`（截断幂律拟合）、`figures/Gonzalez2008_p12_fig1.png`（轨迹地图）、`figures/Gonzalez2008_page3/4.png`（主文分布图整页）

## 2. Wang, Hou & Barth (2019) — Data-Driven Multi-step Demand Prediction for Ride-hailing (CNN)
- **arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.03441
- **本地**: `Wang2019_ridehailing_demand_arXiv1911.03441.pdf`（13 页）
- **对本项目的意义**：**滴滴 GAIA 成都数据集的官方描述**（中国数据集获取路径的实证）+ Layer1 需求场预测的 CNN 基线

**关键原句**：
| p | 原文 | 中文要点 |
|---|---|---|
| p1 | "a CNN-based deep learning model is proposed for multi-step ride-hailing demand prediction using the trip request data in Chengdu, China, offered by DiDi Chuxing" | 成都滴滴请求级数据 |
| p1 | "accurately predicting the ride-hailing pick-up demand at each 1-km by 1-km zone … for every 10 minutes" | **1km 网格 × 10 分钟**需求场——Layer1 粒度参照 |
| p3 | "DiDi receives more than 25 million trip orders … more than 70 TB of new route data" daily | 平台数据规模 |
| p3 | "In 2017, DiDi announced its GAIA Initiative and decided to share a complete sample of the route and ride request data with the academic community" | GAIA 开放计划出处 |
| p3 | "The request dataset contains **7 million ride request records with origin-destination (OD) points** and the trip start and end times" | **700 万条 OD 请求**（我们做中国验证时的可得量级）|
| p3 | 字段表：Order id / Trip start·end time (Unix) / Pick-up longitude (GCJ-02)… | 字段口径（**GCJ-02 坐标系**——中国数据特有的偏移坑）|

**关键图**：`figures/Wang2019_p4_fig1.png`、`Wang2019_page4/7.png`（需求热力图/CNN 结构与结果）

## 3. Han et al. (2025) — MoveGPT: Scaling Mobility Foundation Models with SAMoE
- **arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18670
- **本地**: `MoveGPT_arXiv2505.18670.pdf`（19 页）
- **对本项目的意义**：跨城市迁移的基线方法（研究难点.md 已详查代码）；其"未建模外生条件"正是本项目切入点

**关键原句**：
| p | 原文 | 中文要点 |
|---|---|---|
| p1 | "a unified location encoder that maps geographically disjoint locations into a shared semantic space, enabling pre-training on a global scale" | 统一位置编码 → 跨城预训练 |
| p1 | "Pre-trained on billion-scale datasets … performance gains of up to 35% on average" | 十亿级点位预训练 |
| p2 | "The first barrier is the lack of a universal basic unit, which directly prevents the scaling of data across geographies" | 跨城第一障碍=无统一基本单元 |
| p2 | "maps all locations into a universal semantic feature space based on their geographic, functional, and social characteristics" | 位置=地理+功能(POI)+社会特征 |
| p5 | "we design a flexible and scalable pre-training task based on a retrieval framework" | 检索式输出层（换城市=换候选库）|
| p8 | "exceptional generalization capacity … cross-city knowledge transfer with limited target data requirements" | 少样本迁移结论 |

**关键图**：`figures/MoveGPT_page4.png`（位置编码+SAMoE 框架整页）、`figures/MoveGPT_page8.png`（**Table 1 主结果 + Fig.5 跨城 5% 微调对比**）、`figures/MoveGPT_page9.png`（scaling law）

## 4. Zhu et al. (NeurIPS 2023) — DiffTraj: Generating GPS Trajectory with Diffusion Probabilistic Model
- **arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.11582
- **本地**: `DiffTraj_NeurIPS2023_arXiv2304.11582.pdf`（21 页，15.7MB）
- **对本项目的意义**：方案 A 下游"条件扩散生成器"的方法原型；其条件注入方式（Traj-UNet）是我们伪极端样本要混训对接的接口

**关键原句**：
| p | 原文 | 中文要点 |
|---|---|---|
| p1 | "trajectory generation … replacing original data with generated, privacy-free alternatives" | 生成的动机之一=隐私替代 |
| p1 | "reconstruct and synthesize geographic trajectories from white noise through a reverse trajectory denoising process" | 白噪声→反向去噪→轨迹 |
| p1 | "a Trajectory UNet (Traj-UNet) … to **embed conditional information** and accurately estimate noise levels during the reverse process" | **条件注入机制**（与我们要衔接的接口）|
| p1 | "DiffTraj can be intuitively applied to generate high-fidelity trajectories while **retaining the original distributions**" | 保分布生成（与我们"替身验证比分布"同口径）|
| p2 | "the diffusion model reconstructs data step-by-step from random noise, making it suitable for [human mobility's stochastic characteristics]" | 扩散适配出行的随机性 |
| p2 | "Since the diffusion model generates trajectories from random noise, it eliminates the risk of privacy leakage" | 无隐私泄露 |

**关键图**：`figures/DiffTraj_page4_framework.png`（**Fig.2 总框架**：正向扩散加噪 + Traj-UNet 条件反向）、`figures/DiffTraj_page2_teaser.png`（生成样例对照）、`figures/DiffTraj_page9_results.png`（分布保持评估）
